Our Story

Mike Kafe Ta is a play of words. It may sound “Makkapitta,” meaning “cousins,” or “Mike, kafa Ta,” meaning “Mike, let’s have coffee,” in the Ybanag language.

Mike Kafe Ta began with a simple idea from Mike.

He saw the need for a place where people could pause, talk, and feel at ease—over good coffee and real conversations.

He brought that idea to his three cousins, Tina, Ruby Anne, and Jomary. Together, they built it from the ground up.

Each of them plays a part. Different strengths. One goal. A space that’s honest, familiar, and made for the kind of moments that matter.

Mike Kafe Ta is their way of giving back—through coffee, company, and the connections that stay.

Inspired by Mike. Built with intention. The form reflects balance—strong, steady, and open to everyone.

The Three Dots

Mike’s three cousins: Tina. Ruby Anne. Jomary. Three people. One shared belief. Also a reminder of what matters most—connection, trust, and purpose.

The Coffee Bean

The reason people come in. The detail that ties everything together. Simple, honest, and always worth sharing.
